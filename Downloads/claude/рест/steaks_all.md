## Описания отрубов (для консультации по мясу, EN)

**New York Steak** — cut from the beef short loin, a perfect balance of tenderness and
intense beefy taste. Grilled, broiled or pan-seared to perfection.

**Rib-eye Steak** — from the rib section, renowned for exceptional tenderness and rich
flavor. Generous marbling gives a buttery, melt-in-your-mouth taste.

**Filet Mignon Steak** — cut from the tenderloin, the pinnacle of luxury dining. Unmatched
tenderness, buttery texture and delicate, refined flavor.

**T-bone Steak** — from the beef loin, features both the buttery tenderloin and the savory
strip steak, separated by the distinctive T-shaped bone. Balance of tenderness and flavor.

**Tomahawk Steak** — a ribeye cut with an elongated bone (like a tomahawk axe handle).
Exceptional marbling, rich flavor, melt-in-your-mouth tenderness; a showstopper.

**Machete Steak** — flavorful, versatile cut from the diaphragm muscle. Rich beefy taste,
slightly chewy texture, prominent grain, bold flavor.

**Tenderloin Steak** — premium cut from the tenderloin section. Lean, succulent, minimal
connective tissue; one of the most tender and sought-after cuts.

**Cowboy Steak** — bold, flavorful cut of impressive size. Marbling enhances richness and
tenderness; crispy outer crust and juicy interior. A centerpiece for special occasions.

**Wagyu Bavette** — from the abdominal area of Wagyu cattle (also called flank steak).
Intricate marbling gives luxurious tenderness and rich, buttery flavor.

**Wagyu Kagoshima A5+** — the pinnacle of Wagyu beef, from Kagoshima, Japan. A5+ is the
highest grade; extraordinary marbling, unmatched tenderness, buttery melt-in-your-mouth
texture and a symphony of intricate flavors.
